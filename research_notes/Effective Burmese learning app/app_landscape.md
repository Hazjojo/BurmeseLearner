# Language Learning App and Method Landscape: Mechanics, Evidence, Criticisms (as of Nov 2026)

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[INDEPENDENT]**: peer-reviewed or academic work with no vendor funding found
- **[VENDOR]**: commissioned by, co-authored by, or published by the company
- **[ANECDOTAL]**: Reddit, reviews, press opinion, aggregators, petitions
- **[BUSINESS]**: company financial disclosures or financial press

Research limits: www.sec.gov, investingnews.com and androidauthority.com could not be fetched from this session (blocked by network allowlist), so Duolingo's Q3 2026 figures come from search-result summaries of the shareholder letter and secondary write-ups, not the full letter. I used 18 tool calls, so the 18 products in scope are covered unevenly. Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu, Rosetta Stone and Dreaming Spanish are covered best. Anki/FSRS, Lingvist, Mango, Memrise, Speak, italki and Refold are mostly listed under Gaps.

## 1. Core mechanics per app/method and the evidence on outcomes

### Takeaway
Nearly all outcome evidence for named apps is vendor-funded or vendor-co-authored, uses small or self-selected samples, and measures receptive skills (reading/listening, vocabulary) far more than speaking. The one consistent result across both independent and vendor studies is that **time on task predicts gains**. The design of the app matters less, as far as these studies can show. Beginner apps reliably move learners to Novice High / Intermediate Low (around A1–A2), and no app study shows apps alone producing advanced proficiency.

### Cited Findings

**Duolingo** (core mechanic: short gamified lessons of translation, matching, listening and tapping word-tiles; streaks, XP, leagues; hearts, now "Energy"; AI "Max" tier with video call)
- [VENDOR, peer-reviewed] Jiang, Rollinson, Plonsky, Gustafson & Pajak (2021), *Foreign Language Annals* 54(4): 974–1002. Learners who completed beginning Spanish/French content reached **Intermediate Low in reading and Novice High in listening** (ACTFL). Their scores were "comparable with those of university students at the end of the fourth semester." Authors include Duolingo staff, and Plonsky (NAU) is an academic co-author. — [NAU Experts record](https://experts.nau.edu/en/publications/evaluating-the-reading-and-listening-outcomes-of-beginning-level-/)
- [VENDOR] Duolingo's Jan 2022 press release frames the result as: completing 5 units ≈ 4 university semesters, and 7 units ≈ 5 semesters. Its "Intermediate" framing goes further than the abstract's "Intermediate Low / Novice High". — [Duolingo investor release](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/leading-language-research-journal-publishes-study-showing)
- [VENDOR] Duolingo English-course study (Jiang et al., CALICO Journal): learners at the end of Basic (A2) content scored Intermediate High in reading and listening across three courses. — [CALICO Journal](https://journal.equinoxpub.com/Calico/article/view/26704); [Avant Assessment PDF](https://cdnprodwpv2.avantassessment.com/wp-content/uploads/Duolingo-English-Courses-in-Developing-Proficiency.pdf)
- [VENDOR] A June 2021 Duolingo speaking whitepaper reports most participants reached CEFR A2+ in speaking. — [Scribd copy of Duolingo Speaking Whitepaper](https://www.scribd.com/document/629439838/Duolingo-Speaking-Whitepaper)
- [VENDOR, commissioned] Vesselinov & Grego (2012), funded by Duolingo: about **34 hours** (range 26–49) of Duolingo ≈ one college semester of Spanish. It used volunteers over a two-month window, and the authors cautioned against generalizing to other languages. — [TechCrunch 2013](https://techcrunch.com/2013/01/17/study-learning-spanish-with-duolingo-can-be-more-effective-than-college-classes-or-rosetta-stone/amp); [Scribd: DuolingoReport-Final](https://www.scribd.com/doc/295554832/DuolingoReport-Final)
- [INDEPENDENT critique] Stephen Krashen published a critique of the Vesselinov & Grego study ("Does Duolingo trump university-level language learning?"), noting it was Duolingo-funded. — [Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/doc/311656246/krashen-does-duolingo-trump-pdf)
- [INDEPENDENT] Loewen, Crowther, Isbell, Kim, Maloney, Miller & Rawal (2019), *ReCALL* 31(3): 293–311. **Nine** participants studied Turkish on Duolingo for a semester. They improved on L2 measures, and time spent correlated moderately with gains. Learners liked the flexibility and gamification but reported variable motivation and "frustration with instructional materials." The authors note limited independent research has found problems with persistence, motivation and efficacy. — [Cambridge Core abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/recall/article/abs/mobileassisted-language-learning-a-duolingo-case-study/A4D7C8F71782A37D258C19F357DDBCBE); [PDF](https://cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/A4D7C8F71782A37D258C19F357DDBCBE/S0958344019000065a.pdf/mobileassisted_language_learning_a_duolingo_case_study.pdf)
- [INDEPENDENT] A Cambridge-published study compared classroom-only, Duolingo-only and combined beginner French learners. It found classroom instruction and Duolingo "comparably effective" for beginners. — [Cambridge Core reader](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/68C0E7E296669798089C84CDC7F3BB9E/core-reader)
- [INDEPENDENT, small] A WorldCALL 2023 study of 81 Japanese learners in Duolingo's English course reported results similar to the vendor findings. — [IAFOR PDF](https://papers.iafor.org/wp-content/uploads/papers/worldcall2023/WorldCALL2023_73369.pdf)

**Babbel** (core mechanic: structured lessons with explicit grammar tips, dialogues and review; human-designed curricula; Babbel Live classes)
- [VENDOR co-authored, peer-reviewed] Loewen, Isbell & Sporn (2020), *Foreign Language Annals* 53(2): 209–233. 54 completers (85 enrolled) studied Spanish on Babbel for 12 weeks, targeting about 10 min/day. **About 59% improved at least one ACTFL sublevel on the OPIc oral test**, even though Babbel offered few speaking opportunities. The MSU press account says "almost 69%"; the discrepancy is unresolved. **Study time was the strongest predictor** on all three measures, and motivation predicted oral gains. Sporn is a Babbel employee. — [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2020/06/200609095027.htm); [MSU Today](https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2019/new-study-gives-insight-on-effectiveness-of-language-learning-apps); [PIE News](https://thepienews.com/news/time-determining-factor-language-learning-says-babbel/)
- [VENDOR, commissioned] Vesselinov & Grego (2016, CUNY): truly novice users needed about **15 hours** over two months to cover one college semester of Spanish, and other users about 21 hours. — [Babbel press release](https://press.babbel.com/en/releases/2016-09-29-Efficacy_Study.html)

**Busuu** (core mechanic: structured lessons plus community correction of written and spoken exercises by native speakers)
- [VENDOR] Busuu claims "22 hours of Busuu Premium = 1 college semester." This is its own research page, and the methodology was not verified here. — [Busuu research](https://www.busuu.com/research/university-study)
- A 2025 Busuu study exists (Scribd copy), but its contents were not reviewed. — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/863208084/Busuu-2025-study)

**Rosetta Stone** (core mechanic: "immersion" picture–word matching with no translation, plus speech recognition)
- [VENDOR, commissioned] Vesselinov & Grego (2009): Rosetta Stone users needed **55–60 hours** to cover one semester of material (per TechCrunch). Loewen et al. cite **45 hours** instead, so the two sources conflict. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2013/01/17/study-learning-spanish-with-duolingo-can-be-more-effective-than-college-classes-or-rosetta-stone/amp); [Loewen et al. 2019 PDF](https://cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/A4D7C8F71782A37D258C19F357DDBCBE/S0958344019000065a.pdf/mobileassisted_language_learning_a_duolingo_case_study.pdf)

**Pimsleur, Glossika, Language Transfer, LingQ, Clozemaster** (mechanics and practitioner views only; no independent outcome studies found)
- Pimsleur is audio-first, prompt-and-respond spoken drilling with graduated-interval recall. Clozemaster is fill-in-the-blank (cloze) sentences from large sentence corpora. Clozemaster's own blog says the two target different skills: Pimsleur focuses on speaking, Clozemaster on recognition. It adds that "neither... is designed to take learners to an advanced level." [VENDOR] — [Clozemaster blog](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/?p=7529)
- Glossika drills full sentences with spaced repetition for listening and speaking, and gives "sentences and no rules." Language Transfer is free audio that "explains how the language works" through guided reasoning (the Michel Thomas style). [ANECDOTAL/review site] — [alllanguageresources: Glossika vs Pimsleur](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/glossika-vs-pimsleur/)
- Reddit sentiment summarized by an aggregator [ANECDOTAL, secondhand]: Language Transfer gets the most enthusiasm as free and good for grammar foundations, though one dissenter said it lacks sequenced vocabulary. LingQ is praised as an input/reading tool, usually paired with other resources. Clozemaster is "nice if you know 50-100 words but won't take you so far." Glossika is seen as better for more advanced learners. Anki is "not a full language course" and best for words you keep forgetting. — [GummySearch Reddit aggregator](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/language-learning-app)
- LingQ's blog calls Language Transfer "the closest free match to Pimsleur's audio format" [VENDOR, competitor]. — [LingQ blog](https://www.lingq.com/blog/pimsleur-alternatives/)

**Dreaming Spanish / comprehensible input (CI) video platforms; Refold**
- The mechanic is a graded video library (Superbeginner → Advanced) with hour-based level milestones, built on Krashen's input hypothesis. A review reports the platform recommends roughly **600 hours of input before speaking**, with comfortable conversation around 1,000–1,500 hours [ANECDOTAL/review]. — [FluentU review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/dreaming-spanish/)
- [VENDOR] Dreaming's blog claims CI is "not only necessary... but also sufficient." — [Dreaming blog](https://www.dreaming.com/blog-posts/the-og-immersion-method)
- [ANECDOTAL] r/languagelearning users have questioned the silent-period claim. One commenter noted that most CI research comes from classrooms (storytelling, conversation), not self-study with video. — [r/languagelearning thread, Jun 2025 (libreddit mirror)](https://libreddit.winscloud.net/r/languagelearning/comments/1lei607/what_does_the_research_actually_say_about_the)
- [ANECDOTAL] A review calls Dreaming Spanish "fundamentally a content library, not a learning system" and says speaking is undervalued. — [FluentU review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/dreaming-spanish/); see also [Palteca review](https://palteca.com/dreaming-spanish-review)
- No peer-reviewed study of the Dreaming Spanish program itself was found.

**Praktika / Speak / AI tutor apps**
- The only studies found are small perception studies, mostly Indonesian student theses and journals. They examine *perceptions* of Praktika, not controlled proficiency gains. [INDEPENDENT but weak] — [ELTIN journal](https://e-journal.stkipsiliwangi.ac.id/index.php/eltin/article/view/5857/2212); [IAIN Kediri thesis](https://etheses.iainkediri.ac.id/18452/2/21202119_bab1.pdf)
- [ANECDOTAL] Trustpilot reviewers of Praktika report it is "great for listening but not for speaking" and that lesson level was mismatched to proficiency. — [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/praktika.ai?page=6)
- No independent efficacy study of Speak was found.

### Inferences
- Vendor "hours per college semester" figures (Babbel 15–21 h, Busuu 22 h, Duolingo 34 h, Rosetta Stone 45–60 h) come from comparable commissioned designs, mostly by the same researchers (Vesselinov & Grego). They are not comparable to independent RCTs, and they compare against a weak baseline (first-semester college coursework, which itself yields low proficiency).
- Babbel and Duolingo both produced *some* oral gain without much speaking practice, but at only about one sublevel. Speaking appears to be the least well-served skill in mainstream apps.
- Time on task being the dominant predictor implies that engagement mechanics matter, but only to the extent they produce real study time rather than token streak-saving sessions.

### Gaps
- No outcome studies were found for Anki/shared decks (as products), Pimsleur, Glossika, LingQ, Language Transfer/Michel Thomas, Clozemaster, Memrise, Lingvist, Mango, Speak, italki or Refold. These were not searched individually because of the call budget.
- Pooled effect sizes from recent MALL meta-analyses were not retrieved (see section 3).
- Duolingo Max video call: no efficacy data found.

## 2. Retention and engagement data; effects of streaks, leagues and notifications; the gamification critique

### Takeaway
Duolingo's engagement machine works on its own metrics. DAU growth reaccelerated to 29% YoY in Q3 2026, driven mostly by retention experiments on notifications and streaks. That is evidence about *engagement*, not learning. The 2025 "AI-first" backlash coincided with a sharp DAU growth slowdown, which shows that brand trust is part of retention. Streak data shows habit persistence, but no source found ties streaks to proficiency outcomes.

## 3. Which mechanics correlate with real gains

### Takeaway
The strongest independent evidence supports **spaced practice** (a medium-to-large effect on L2 learning) and **time on task**. Within the app studies, consistent study time and motivation predict gains, while gamification per se has weak causal evidence. For audio-first prompted production, speaking with feedback, and graded input, there are no app-specific independent trials. The case for them rests on general SLA theory and practitioner reports.

### Cited Findings
- [INDEPENDENT] Kim & Webb (2022), *Language Learning* 72(1): 269–319. A meta-analysis of 98 effect sizes from 48 experiments (N = 3,411) found spacing had a **medium-to-large effect** on L2 learning. Short spacing equalled long spacing on immediate tests but **fell behind on delayed tests**. **Equal and expanding schedules performed about the same.** Moderators included learning target, number of sessions, practice type, feedback timing and retention interval. — [British Library record](https://eld.bl.uk/catalog/100152978253_0x000001); [UB repository](https://diposit.ub.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/911f8da8-9666-4917-9411-c9ae6754cd7b/content); [SSLLT follow-up review](https://dx.doi.org/10.14746/ssllt.2022.12.3.2)
- [VENDOR co-authored] In Babbel's 12-week study, time on app was the strongest predictor of vocabulary, grammar and oral gains. — [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2020/06/200609095027.htm)
- [INDEPENDENT] In Duolingo (Loewen 2019), time spent correlated moderately with gains (n = 9). — [Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/recall/article/abs/mobileassisted-language-learning-a-duolingo-case-study/A4D7C8F71782A37D258C19F357DDBCBE)
- [INDEPENDENT] Gamification reviews find positive associations but thin causal evidence. — [Luo 2023, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10135444/)
- [VENDOR] Duolingo's 2021 reading and listening results put learners at Intermediate Low reading but only Novice High listening. — [NAU Experts](https://experts.nau.edu/en/publications/evaluating-the-reading-and-listening-outcomes-of-beginning-level-/)

### Inferences
- The finding that expanding and equal intervals perform about the same suggests a lean app does not need a sophisticated scheduler to get most of the spacing benefit. What matters is that reviews are spaced and delayed retention is targeted. A fancier scheduler such as FSRS may still improve review efficiency, but that was not researched here.
- Mainstream apps are weakest at listening and speaking, which suggests audio-first design and speaking production are where a lean app can differentiate. This rests on the observed gaps, not on trials proving those mechanics.

### Gaps
- No independent RCTs were found for: audio-first prompt/response (Pimsleur-style), sentence-mining SRS versus word-list SRS, AI speaking feedback, or graded-input video, each as an app mechanic.
- Pooled effect sizes from the 2024 Soler Santaliestra et al. MALL meta-analysis were not retrieved.
- No evidence was gathered on FSRS versus SM-2 (Anki) efficiency.

## 4. Common user complaints

### Takeaway
The most-documented complaints concern Duolingo's monetization mechanics. The 2025 switch from Hearts to "Energy", which drains even on correct answers for free users, drew petitions and churn stories. Other complaints are AI-generated content and course overhauls (2025–2026), and frustration with instructional materials. CI platforms are criticized for neglecting speaking, and AI tutors for mismatched level.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR] Duolingo replaced Hearts with Energy, saying Hearts (each mistake cost one heart for free users) "was not the most effective way to support learning." — [Duolingo blog: Energy](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)
- [ANECDOTAL] Free users start with 25 energy, which drains on correct answers as well as wrong ones. Super/Max subscribers get unlimited energy. Multiple Change.org petitions call for the old system back or for removing both. — [Duolingo Fandom Wiki](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy); [Change.org petition](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy); [Change.org petition 2](https://www.change.org/p/restore-the-hearts-feature-in-duolingo-and-remove-energy); [NuclearMonster, May 2025](https://nuclearmonster.com/2025/05/duolingo-switches-from-hearts-to-energy-adds-chess)
- [ANECDOTAL/press] Android Authority writers published "quitting Duolingo" pieces over the energy system and over "broken" updated courses, plus a reader poll on course updates. These were not fetched; titles only. — [Quitting over energy](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/); [Broken updated courses](https://www.androidauthority.com/duolingo-broken-updated-courses-why-i-quit-3686405/); [Poll results](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-course-updates-poll-results-3690803)
- [ANECDOTAL/press] A PCWorld writer argues "Duolingo got easier when I paid for it — that was the problem," saying the paid tier removes the friction that drove effort. — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2924235/duolingo-got-easier-when-i-paid-for-it-that-was-the-problem.html)
- [INDEPENDENT] Learners reported "frustration with instructional materials." — [Loewen et al. 2019](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/recall/article/abs/mobileassisted-language-learning-a-duolingo-case-study/A4D7C8F71782A37D258C19F357DDBCBE)
- [ANECDOTAL] Dreaming Spanish reviewers say speaking is undervalued and call the platform a content library rather than a system. — [FluentU](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/dreaming-spanish/)
- [ANECDOTAL] Praktika reviewers report the AI lesson level is mismatched and say it is "great for listening but not for speaking." — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/praktika.ai?page=6)

### Inferences
- Paywalling mistakes or practice volume (hearts/energy) is the most resented mechanic. A lean app should never limit how much a learner can practice.
- Practice friction that scales with effort (more correct answers drain more energy) inverts the learning incentive and should be avoided.

### Gaps
- Not verified with sources in this pass: removal of Duolingo sentence discussion forums (2022), robotic TTS complaints, translation-only exercises, and ads complaints. Searches did not surface primary sources.
- No direct r/languagelearning consensus thread was fetched. Reddit sentiment here is secondhand.

## 5. Minimal effective toolkit recommended by experienced self-learners

### Takeaway
Secondhand Reddit sentiment points to a recurring combination: a free structured audio course (Language Transfer) for foundations, SRS (Anki) for words you keep forgetting, an input tool (LingQ, or graded CI video), and eventually speaking with humans or tutors. Each tool is seen as partial. No single app is regarded as sufficient.

### Cited Findings
- [ANECDOTAL] Language Transfer is the most enthusiastically recommended free tool. LingQ is used for input "paired with other resources." Clozemaster is for early vocabulary breadth. Anki is "not a full language course." — [GummySearch aggregator](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/language-learning-app); [italki "best apps per Reddit" (vendor-adjacent)](https://www.italki.com/en/blog/best-language-learning-apps-reddit)
- [VENDOR] Clozemaster says many learners pair Pimsleur (speaking) with Clozemaster (recognition). — [Clozemaster blog](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/?p=7529)
- [ANECDOTAL] CI advocates report understanding most native media after about 1,000 input hours. — [FluentU review](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/reviews/dreaming-spanish/)

### Inferences
- The minimal toolkit maps to four functions: (1) a guided audio foundation with prompted production, (2) spaced recall of high-frequency items, (3) large amounts of graded input, and (4) speaking with feedback. A lean app could combine 1–3 and hand off 4.

### Gaps
- No primary r/languagelearning FAQ or wiki was fetched. The Refold roadmap specifics were not retrieved.

## 6. Lean/minimalist app design lessons

### Takeaway
I found little direct sourcing on products praised for simplicity. The indirect lessons come from the backlash material. Users resent practice caps, paywalled friction and course churn, while praising free, focused single-purpose tools (Language Transfer, Anki, Clozemaster, CI libraries).

### Cited Findings
- [ANECDOTAL] Single-purpose tools (Language Transfer, Clozemaster, Anki, LingQ) are recommended for one job each, not as complete systems. — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/language-learning-app)
- [VENDOR] Streak Freeze shows that flexibility in habit mechanics can retain users without punishing missed days. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)
- [ANECDOTAL] Energy, a monetization-driven practice cap, is the most-protested Duolingo change. — [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy)

### Inferences
- Copy: daily streak with freeze, short sessions, spaced review and audio. Skip: leagues, gems, energy/hearts, cosmetic currencies, and mini-games unrelated to language. Duolingo added chess in 2025 ([NuclearMonster](https://nuclearmonster.com/2025/05/duolingo-switches-from-hearts-to-energy-adds-chess)).

### Gaps
- No design case studies or HN threads on minimalist language apps were retrieved.

## 7. Less-commonly-taught, tonal and non-Latin-script languages (Burmese focus)

### Takeaway
Burmese is poorly served. Duolingo has no Burmese course (per a competitor's claim). Options are mostly phrasebooks, a handwriting tracer, and Ling, whose tone and script claims are its own marketing. For a learner, the main hurdles are tone minimal pairs (e.g., နာ "pain" vs နား "ear") and the script. That suggests tone-contrast audio drills and stroke/character trainers are high-value features.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR, competitor] Ling's blog says Duolingo does not offer Burmese. Ling advertises Burmese script and tones with "visual guides and audio drills" and finger-tracing script practice. — [Ling: Burmese app](https://ling-app.com/blog/burmese-language-app/); [Ling: learn Burmese](https://ling-app.com/learn-burmese/)
- App Store options: "Learn Burmese Handwriting" ($1.99, stroke-by-stroke script); "Learn Burmese" (free, 500+ native-voiced phrases in 20 categories); "Simply Learn Burmese" (phonetic + script phrasebook); MMSpeaker (phonetic spellings). — [App Store id1425465073](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1425465073); [App Store id6446809430](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6446809430); [Simply Learn Burmese](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EB%AC%B4%EB%A3%8C%EB%A1%9C-%EB%AF%B8%EC%96%80%EB%A7%88%EC%96%B4-%EB%B0%B0%EC%9A%B0%EA%B8%B0-%EB%AF%B8%EC%96%80%EB%A7%88%EC%96%B4-%EC%96%B4%ED%9C%98/id1119979097); [MMSpeaker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id686918099)
- [ANECDOTAL] Preply notes tone confusion, such as နာ (level tone, "pain") vs နား (high/heavy tone, "ear"), and recommends listening for pitch movement. — [Preply](https://preply.com/en/blog/why-learn-burmese)

### Inferences
- Because Burmese has no major-app competitor, a focused app with native audio, tone minimal-pair drills, and a script trainer tied to SRS would fill a real gap.

### Gaps
- No evidence was gathered on pitch visualizers or tone-training efficacy (e.g., HVPT, high-variability phonetic training), or on how Mandarin, Thai or Vietnamese apps implement tone features.
- No independent reviews comparing the Burmese apps were found.
